Privacy

Privacy

Updated November 16, 2026

Chanclazo is a free browser game. There is no account and no real money. We do not sell personal information.

What we measure

We use Google Analytics to see which pages and games people open, when a game starts, the first tap, and about how long a visit lasts. Some games also send the wave or bonus you used. Analytics sets a cookie in your browser so it can tell a return visit from a new one. We ask Google to shorten IP addresses. We do not use this for ads.

What stays on your device

Best scores, sound settings, and an X handle you type are saved in this browser. They are not sent anywhere unless you choose to save a score.

Leaderboards

On Chancla Strike and Match, Save score sends the X handle you typed, the score, and the level to our leaderboard. If that save cannot be stored, the score stays on this device only. Share on X opens X. We do not post for you.

Kids

The games are made for a general audience. We do not ask for a name, email, or age.
