One abuela. One chancla. A lot of calaveras.
Phone: drag the left side of the screen to move and the right side to aim. Abuela throws chanclas nonstop in the direction you aim. Computer: move with WASD and aim with the mouse, then click or hold to throw (arrow keys also aim and throw). Sugar skulls swarm in from every side. You have 3 hearts. After each wave, pick an upgrade. Survive as long as you can and beat your high score!
