One abuela. One chancla. A lot of calaveras.
Phone: drag the left side of the screen to move and the right side to aim. Abuela throws chanclas nonstop in the direction you aim. Tap the 🌀 button for a spinning chancla whirl that smashes nearby skulls. Computer: move with WASD and aim with the mouse, then click or hold to throw (arrow keys also aim and throw). Right-click or E does the chancla whirl. Sugar skulls swarm in from every side and get bigger every wave. You have 3 hearts. After each wave, pick an upgrade. Beat the boss to clear the level. Smashed skulls drop gold coins; spend them in the Shop on upgrades and new characters. Clear all 3 levels to win!
